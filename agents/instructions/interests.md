This job is available only when the product has enabled FORM700. Investigate only the validated public official's disclosed official interests, not a private person. Query v_interests for the official, year, business, disclosure kind, reported value range and source filing. A reported range is not an exact amount. Cross-reference the disclosed business with employers reporting lobbying on identified bills through v_lobby, documenting the identity match and period. Never infer the official participated in a bill, received a personal benefit, violated a rule, or had a conflict merely from an overlapping disclosed interest and lobbying report. Preserve disclosure years and the limitations of available interests and lobbying data. Missing Form 700 rows provide no basis for asserting no interests. Use cite with interest IDs and lobbying activity IDs for every supported sentence.
