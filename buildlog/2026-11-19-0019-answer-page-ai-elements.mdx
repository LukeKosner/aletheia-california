## Shipped

- **Answer page on AI Elements:** the question box is `PromptInput` with `Suggestion` chips. Progress is `ChainOfThought` steps with `Shimmer`, worded for any question ("Researching", "Checking every citation"). Each sentence gets one quiet citation pill ("8 filings", "sos.ca.gov") that opens an `InlineCitation` hover card. A follow-up "Ask another question" box sits under the answer.
- **Sources you can read:** filings are labeled in plain English: form type, filer, period, filing date and CAL‑ACCESS, for example "Lobbying firm report · KP Public Affairs · Jan–Mar 2025". The server sends these labels in its first response. The list is collapsed under "Based on N public filings". Filings with the same type and filer collapse into one line of date chips. The legend, the numbered list and the "[no source]" tags are gone. Internal keys such as `calaccess:filer:…` are stripped from answer text.
- **Lobbying filings name their firm:** `getFiling` in `packages/tools` now returns the employer and firm on each lobbying line (`lobby_parties`) and the lobbying period.
- **Money map on the answer page:** it is drawn from data already loaded, with one line per giver-and-receiver pair and amounts grouped by pair. Raw filing UUIDs are gone. Page scroll is no longer captured by the map. `/i/[id]/map` still works for shared links.
- **Fewer duplicate runs:** the answer page sends each question once. Before, React's development double-mount started two paid investigations per question.
- **Fast reopen:** a finished agent run and each public filing are cached with `"use cache"` (`app/api/investigations/cached.ts`). Unpublishing is still checked on every request.
- **Agent instructions** (`agents/instructions/ask.md`, live after the next harness sync):
  - "Who funds a measure" covers committee contributors, not only independent expenditures.
  - Process questions use the official explainer.
  - `insight` is never used for an unsourced gap.
  - No internal ids in sentence text.
  - Government studies never ask party or voting plan.
- **Interviews and studies:**
  - Payouts and their actions are staff-only.
  - Study results and study answers are visible only to the browser that created the study, and staff. Transcripts are visible only to the voter and staff.
  - Consent says where data goes: the general interview is published without names; a study is shared with its sponsor and not published.
  - The builder rejects banned and identifying wording with a message instead of writing "[removed]".
  - Study quotas count finished or recent interviews. A reward is recorded only after a real conversation (4 voter turns).
  - A closed study link says so. The business example is a real business question.
  - Ask's study drafts open the builder fully prefilled (title, goal, questions, profile fields, custom field, size), with organization left for the sponsor. The draft's describing sentences no longer carry an "Interviews" pill. The builder says the sponsor shares the link, rather than implying that Aletheia recruits people.
- **Study follow-on after the voter interview:** when a general interview is saved, the voter's thank-you page may offer one open study: "One more, if you'd like". The card names the sponsor and its kind, says it is a separate interview of about ten minutes for another $5, and says answers go only to the sponsor and are not published. Taking it starts a new interview tied to that study, with its own consent and its own reward. Its answers stay out of public insights. County, age, party, voting plan and email carry over only to the voter's own browser (owner cookie); consent never does. Each email can take a study once. Only business studies are offered (`FOLLOW_ON_KINDS`); government studies wait until government sponsors are verified. The oldest open study with interviews still needed goes first.
- **Demo data:** `scripts/reset_interview_data.py --yes --all` now also clears every study (business and government) plus the payment and payout records; Stripe's own test history is untouched. `scripts/seed_sample_interviews.py` now adds 30 sample voter interviews (27 counties, with sources, a quote and a message to leaders) and 3 sample studies with 10 answers between them (a city planning department, a coffee roaster and a solar installer). Every row is invented, labeled "Sample", and uses `sample@example.invalid`, so no reward can be sent. At the owner's request the dev database was cleared (15 interviews, 4 studies, 7 test-payment records) and reseeded.

- The `getFiling` lobbying change is untested against a database here; it was checked in the browser on the AB 1018 answer.
