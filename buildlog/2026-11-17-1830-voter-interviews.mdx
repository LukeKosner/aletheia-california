## Shipped

- **Separate `interview` Postgres schema** (migration 0004, `pgSchema("interview")`): `session`, `research`, `payment`, `payout`. Nothing in `public` changes. `scripts/provision_interview_db.py` (human step) applies the migration and creates an `interview_app` role that can select, insert and update only these four tables, then writes `INTERVIEW_DATABASE_URL` to `apps/web/.env.local`. `drizzle.config.ts` now filters `public` and `interview`.
- **`INTERVIEW_PREVIEW` flag** (default off). When it is on, `/interview/*` and `/api/interview/*` skip team sign-in for the local preview and the header shows an "Interview" link. Every other page stays gated; `PUBLIC_SITE` stays off.
- `/interview`: public-interview consent, then county (all 58), age range, party preference (Secretary of State list) and voting plan.
- `/interview/[id]`: live voice interview on **OpenAI GPT-Live (`gpt-live-1`)** over WebRTC. The browser posts its SDP offer to `/api/interview/sessions/[id]/connect`, and the server calls `POST /v1/live/sessions`, so the API key never reaches the browser. The `submit_interview` tool runs on a delegated Responses backend (`gpt-5.6-terra`). Its call arrives as `response.event` → `response.output_item.done`, and the result goes back with `response.item.create` plus `response.create`. The session opens with `session.commentary.append` so the interviewer greets first. Typed answers use `session.thinking.append` plus `session.commentary.append`, because GPT-Live has no text turn. Without a microphone, a near-silent track is sent, since GPT-Live needs outgoing audio before it speaks. Setting `OPENAI_REALTIME_MODEL=gpt-realtime*` falls back to the Realtime API. The server validates the tool output against 21 fixed issue tags, clamps lengths and filters banned words.
- **Research** reuses the existing HarnessRouter investigation pipeline (`prepare` and `consume`, general harness): the same scope check, MCP-only data access and cited-claim validation. Each run is linked by `interview.research.investigation_id`. Voter research covers up to three of the voter's questions after payment. Periodic research takes the week's top two issues and skips any researched in the last day. It runs through `/api/interview/cron` (needs `CRON_SECRET` outside development) or the "Run periodic research now" button.
- `/interview/insights`: a public statewide analysis with interview and county counts, top issues as bars (filterable by county and party), the top three issues per county, recent voter questions and recent interviews, plus the periodic and voter research lists.
- **Paywall (Stripe test mode only; the code refuses any key other than `sk_test_` or `rk_test_`):** report summaries are free. Full cited sections and sources need a reports pass ($2.99). Generating research costs $4.99 and includes the pass. Checkout is created through Stripe REST with inline `price_data` (no new dependency). The success URL retrieves the session from Stripe, checks `payment_status`, records the payment once (unique on the checkout id), starts research and sets a signed 30-day pass cookie that is checked against the paid payment row.
- `/interview/payouts`: Stripe test balance (available and pending), interview sales, a "top up" button that uses Stripe's `pm_card_bypassPending` test method so funds are available immediately, and a "pay out" button that calls `POST /v1/payouts`. Payments and payouts are listed from the database.

- **UI pass** following the earlier simple-UI direction ("too much extra text", "go back to basics"): shorter copy everywhere, a 4-field form plus one consent checkbox, plain result lists, and payment prompts as single cards. It uses shadcn components (added `checkbox`, `label`, `native-select`, `field`, `alert`, `empty`, `item`; files only, no new packages) along with the existing `card`, `badge`, `collapsible` and `spinner`. Data pages are wrapped in `<Suspense>` and call `connection()` to fit the app's cache-components mode.
- Payout errors explain the fix when the Stripe sandbox has no bank account.
- **Studies, the paid product for governments and businesses:** a sponsor writes up to six questions, pays $149 in Stripe test mode for 20 voice interviews ($5 of each goes to the person interviewed), and shares a link. The GPT-Live interviewer asks the sponsor's questions as written and in a balanced way (a test rephrased "Would you support…" as "support or oppose"), skips party and voting questions for government sponsors, and the study page shows "N of 20" and one answer per question. Migrations 0005 (`interview.study`, `session.study_id`, `session.answers`) and 0006 (`session.reward_email`) were applied with `scripts/provision_interview_db.py`.
- **Finish is deterministic:** GPT-Live sometimes didn't delegate the save (in one test it said "Saving your responses now" and never called the backend). Finish now posts the transcript, and the server makes a forced `submit_interview` call to the Responses API. A save the voice model triggers on its own still works.
- **Adversarial UX review:** three read-only subagents played a first-time voter, a city housing staffer and a hackathon judge. Applied fixes: an email field so the $5 can actually be paid, study pricing that covers its own rewards ($149 for 20, previously $49 with no cap), who pays shown on the home cards, admin buttons moved off the public results page onto a Team page, paid research unlocking its own reports, a heads-up before the mic prompt, no Back link during a call, the no-mic notice moved into the answer field, cancelled and unpaid checkout states for studies, and an error instead of silently dropping a seventh question. Deliberately not applied, because the owner already decided otherwise: making interviews non-public, and replacing the red banner.
- Home is now one headline ("Ask California anything."), two buttons and three "who are you" cards with prices; Legislators, Measures and Issues moved to the footer.
- Earlier home page (superseded): the name's meaning ("Greek for truth. Literally, 'nothing hidden.'") and pronunciation, the headline "See who's behind California politics.", Ask and $5-interview buttons, three audiences (voters, newsrooms, advocates and businesses), a three-step "How it works", and Free / Reports ($2.99) / Research ($4.99) plans mapped to the products that already check out through Stripe. A small red "Beta · Test mode · All money is fake" banner replaces the test-mode notes on individual pages, and a dismissible bottom-right card offers $5 for an interview. A completed interview records a $5 reward as owed.

## Broke or learned

- Tested end to end in the local preview: create interview → GPT-Live greeting → typed answers → Finish → delegated `submit_interview` saved issues, a question, a view and a summary → Stripe sandbox Checkout ($4.99, test card) → return page queued research → HarnessRouter general harness finished in about 6 minutes with 16 cited CAL-ACCESS filings (2026 Prop 1 housing bond committees) → full report visible because the research payment grants the pass. Test top-up of $25 succeeded.
- **Payouts are blocked by the Stripe sandbox setup:** `POST /v1/payouts` returns "no external accounts in usd", and Stripe Connect is not enabled (account creation is refused). Paying out needs a test bank account added in the Stripe dashboard. Paying voters or interviewers needs Connect enabled.
- `gpt-live-1` is rejected by `/v1/realtime/calls` ("not supported in realtime mode"); it only works through `/v1/live/sessions`. `session.instructions.append` alone does not start a spoken turn.
- The interview schema was applied with `scripts/provision_interview_db.py` at the owner's request (2026-11-17). Drizzle's migration journal table was not updated, the same as the earlier hand-provisioned migrations.
- Voice input was not tested: the preview browser has no microphone. Typed input and spoken output were tested.
