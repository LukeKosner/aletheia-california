# data/manual

Hand-checked files. A human approves each row before anything loads it.

## legislator_committees.csv — approved 2026-11-15

Links each 2025–2026 California legislator to CAL-ACCESS candidate-controlled committees.

- Generated by `uv run python -m aletheia_ingest.leginfo committees` (in `py/ingest`) from
  `data/raw/calaccess/dbwebexport.zip` (CAL-ACCESS export, sha256 in `data/raw/MANIFEST.csv`) and the
  staged legislators from `pubinfo_2025.zip`.
- One row per (legislator, committee). Committees are filers with `CMTTE_TYPE = 'C'` on Form
  460/450/497/465/425 cover pages dated 2023-01-01 or later whose candidate name matches the legislator.
- `committee_filer_id` is the CAL-ACCESS FILER_ID (look it up by ID in the Secretary of State's campaign finance search).
- `latest_filing_id` is the most recent cover page (FILING_ID) behind the match, to open as evidence.
- `candidate_filer_ids_f501` is the candidate's own FILER_ID from Form 501.
- `match_basis`:
  - `name + current office + district`: candidate name, office (ASM/SEN) and district match the member's seat.
  - `name + current office, other district`: same chamber, different district (often redistricting or an older committee).
  - `name only (committee office X)`: same name, other office (e.g. LTG, ATT, BOE, city council). **Most likely to be wrong.**
  - `; name variant`: matched on a nickname (Tom/Thomas) or compound surname (Ortega/Ortega-Toro).
- `approved`: `yes` or `no`. Reviewed by the project owner on 2026-11-15: the 316 `name + current office + district`
  rows are `yes`; the other 79 are `no` until someone checks them one by one. Only `yes` rows are loaded.
- Loaded by `load` onto each legislator entity's `attrs` as `calaccess_committee_filer_ids` and
  `calaccess_candidate_filer_ids` (no link table exists yet).
- `committees` refuses to overwrite this file once it has approvals (`--force` to regenerate; approvals are lost).

Not included: general-purpose or ballot-measure committees not controlled by the candidate (`CMTTE_TYPE` other than `C`),
and federal committees (not in CAL-ACCESS).
